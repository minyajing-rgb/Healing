## 6. Canonical QA gates / 验收门

A unit cannot become `PUBLISHED` until:
- source is attached;
- tradition/claim/evidence are separated;
- safety fields are reviewed;
- cultural attribution is specific enough;
- bilingual names are verified;
- map coordinates do not reveal protected/sacred locations;
- endangered plants/animals have conservation notes where relevant;
- no metaphysical claim is rewritten as biomedical fact.
