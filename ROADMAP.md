# ROADMAP / 地球疗愈路线图

## North Star

Build the most navigable bilingual global healing atlas possible:

**database-first → map/timeline-first → AI-native → immersive → offline field network**

---

## Phase 0 — Foundation / 0–4 weeks

**Goal:** define the canonical universe before mass content generation.

Deliver:
- global bilingual taxonomy
- status model
- evidence & safety policy
- cultural-rights model
- core JSON schema
- map/timeline relationship model
- visual design system
- source registry
- first 100–300 seed entities as discovery records
- duplicate/alias strategy

Exit criteria:
- every new item can fit the schema;
- claims can be separated by type/evidence;
- map locations and timeline dates have explicit precision;
- Chinese/English field rules are fixed.

---

## Phase 1 — Core Database / 1–3 months

Build dense starter datasets:

### Chinese cluster
中医 · 中草药 · 针灸 · 艾灸 · 药石 · 食疗 · 药浴 · 瑶浴 · 易经/阴阳五行/节气思想 · 民族医药

### Global plant/aroma cluster
European herbalism · Ayurveda · Unani · Kampo · Korean · Thai · Jamu · selected African ethnobotany · Amazonian/Andean public-source records · Māori/public Pacific sources

### Sensory & mind-body cluster
Aromatherapy · meditation · breath · qigong · yoga · sound/chant · music · forest/garden · animal-assisted

### Ritual/folk cluster
Publicly documented European folk healing / witchcraft-associated healing history, curanderismo and community-specific ritual traditions with rights controls

Target:
- 1,000+ structured entities
- 3,000+ sourced relationships
- bilingual common names
- no “全集已完成” claim yet

Each module:
story → object cards → map → timeline → source → quiz/explore → related journey

---

## Phase 4 — AI Native / 4–8 months

**Ask Earth Healing**

RAG/graph answers must expose:
- source
- claim type
- evidence level
- safety
- cultural context
- uncertainty

Example:
> “Compare lavender in European herbalism, modern aromatherapy and clinical research.”

AI response should not collapse these into one claim.

---

## Phase 5 — 3D & Sensory / 6–12 months

- 3D Earth
- 3D plant specimens
- body layer switch: modern anatomy vs traditional maps
- apothecary lab interactions
- distillation simulation
- bath/thermal scenes
- sound waveform/spectrogram experiences

---

## Phase 6 — Offline / 9–18 months

Public-facing network:
- botanical gardens
- museums
- heritage sites
- public thermal/bath culture
- universities/research institutions
- exhibitions
- fragrance/apothecary workshops
- nature and garden experiences
- field interviews with permission

Clinical practice is a separate workstream and must follow local licensing, training, insurance and safety requirements.
