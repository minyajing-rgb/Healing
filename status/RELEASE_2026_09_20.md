## Boundaries

The previous 500 generated story shells are NOT 500 completed articles. Structural graph edges are NOT verified historical transmission claims. The website does not claim that the global research database is complete.

The time dimension changes visible records and stories; it does not fabricate changing historical political borders. Historical documentation, institutional milestones, research funding and heritage-recognition dates are labelled separately. Recognition dates are not treated as origin dates.

Four earlier image proxies and two video proxies failed decoding and were excluded from publication. Three valid 160x90, 10-second concept clips are labelled previews, not HD masters. Original source-media archival remains a separate task.

## Future updates and domain

Editable production sources are in web/. Reviewed public stories are in data/published-stories.json. Changes to these watched paths on main trigger the build/test/publish workflow. This is automatic publication after edits, not autonomous research or medical review.

No custom domain has been supplied or bound. Relative resource paths support both the current /Healing/ project URL and a future custom domain. Existing publication-branch CNAME values are preserved during builds unless an explicit replacement is supplied.
